Evgeny Kazantsev is currently imprisoned in Russia and lives with a serious orthopedic condition that causes chronic pain and significant mobility issues. His condition affects his ability to walk and function day to day, and it continues to worsen without proper medical support.

According to an official doctor’s note, provided with an English translation, Evgeny urgently needs custom orthopedic footwear. Before this can be produced, an orthopedic specialist must be brought to the prison colony to examine him and conduct the required X-ray diagnostics. These steps are medically necessary and cannot be replaced with standard issue footwear or remote consultations.

Amount needed: 50,000 rubles (approximately $550 USD)  
This amount covers the specialist’s visit to the colony and all required diagnostic procedures.
